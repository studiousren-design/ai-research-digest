## Wider Field

**[OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Cyber crosses the "High" cyber-capability threshold]** — Around 10 August 2026, OpenAI expanded its Daybreak initiative with GPT-5.6-Cyber, a model aimed at advanced cybersecurity queries that autonomously discovered two previously unknown vulnerabilities in Chrome's V8 engine, making it the first OpenAI model to hit the "High" rating under the company's Preparedness Framework for cyber capability.
*Why it might matter later:* An AI system independently finding real, unreported zero-days shifts offensive/defensive security economics — cheap, automated vulnerability discovery could outpace patch cycles well before it's clear whether defenders or attackers benefit more from the same capability.
